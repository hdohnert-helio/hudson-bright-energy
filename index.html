How we help

We don't sell or install solar systems. Our job is to make sure the one you choose is the right one, at a fair price, from a contractor you can trust.

Quote review & comparison

A side-by-side review of your installer proposals: price per watt, equipment, warranties, production estimates and contract red flags.

Financing guidance

Cash vs. loan vs. lease or PPA, how incentives and your federal tax situation may apply, and the trade-offs explained in plain English.

Battery & storage advice

Whether storage makes sense for your home, how to size it, and what to realistically expect during an outage.

Installer vetting & project oversight

Help choosing a contractor, then keeping the project on track from signed contract all the way to permission to operate (PTO).

How it works

Three simple steps, and no pressure at any of them.

  1. Free intro call

    Tell us about your home, your goals and where you are in the process. We'll tell you honestly whether we can help.

  2. We review your home and quotes

    We look at your energy use, roof and site, and any proposals you've received, and dig into the details that matter.

  3. You get a clear recommendation

    A straightforward written summary of your options, what we'd watch out for, and what we'd do in your position.

Why independent advice matters

Most solar advice comes from the people selling the system. That isn't necessarily bad advice, but it is never neutral. Salespeople are paid to close, and the cheapest quote, the most expensive one and the right one are rarely the same.

Hudson Bright Energy is paid by homeowners, not installers or lenders. We don't earn commissions on equipment or financing, so our only goal is a system that makes sense for your home and your budget.

  • Paid by our clients, not by installers or lenders
  • No commissions on equipment or financing
  • Plain-English explanations, not sales pitches
  • Recommendations in writing, so you can take your time
