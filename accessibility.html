Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Hudson Bright Energy wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who rely on assistive technology.

Our standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. This site was built with that standard in mind, including:

  • semantic HTML with a logical heading structure;
  • a "skip to content" link and full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators;
  • text and background colors that meet AA contrast requirements;
  • labels on every form field, with clear error messages;
  • layouts that work on screens from small phones to large desktops, and at up to 200% zoom; and
  • respect for your device's reduced-motion setting.

Report a barrier

If you have trouble using any part of this site, please tell us through our contact form. Let us know the page and what went wrong, and we'll work to fix it and get you the information you need in another way in the meantime.
