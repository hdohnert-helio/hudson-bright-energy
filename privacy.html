Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains what information Hudson Bright Energy ("we", "us") collects through this website and during consulting engagements, how we use it, and the choices you have. We've tried to keep it short and in plain English.

Who we are and how to reach us

Hudson Bright Energy is an independent residential solar consulting business serving homeowners in Connecticut, New York, Massachusetts and New Jersey. The best way to contact us about anything in this policy is the contact form on our home page.

What we collect

  • Information you submit through our contact form: your name, email address, town and state, the services you're interested in, and your message.
  • Information you choose to share during an engagement: for example, utility bills, installer quotes, contracts or photos of your home.

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers, and it loads no third-party scripts or fonts.

How the contact form works

Form submissions are processed by Formspree, which delivers them to us by email. Formspree may process technical data such as your IP address and may use spam-protection tools, which can include Google reCAPTCHA. Formspree's handling of that data is described in its own privacy policy.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data, such as IP addresses, for security and operational purposes. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

How we use your information

We use your information only to respond to your inquiry and, if you engage us, to deliver our consulting services. We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for targeted advertising, and we do not use it for profiling.

When we share information

We share personal information only:

  • with service providers we need to run the business, such as Formspree and our email provider;
  • when you ask us to, for example sharing your details with an installer you have chosen; or
  • when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it to respond to you or complete an engagement, and as long as required for legal and tax recordkeeping. After that, we delete it.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so please don't send sensitive information such as Social Security or bank account numbers through the contact form.

Your privacy rights

Residents of Connecticut, New Jersey and other states with consumer privacy laws have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about them;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • have their information deleted;
  • receive a copy of their information in a portable format; and
  • opt out of the sale of personal information and targeted advertising (we don't do either).

We extend these same rights to every visitor, regardless of where you live.

To make a request, use our contact form and write "Privacy request" in your message, along with what you'd like us to do. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it. We will respond within 45 days.

To appeal, if we decline your request, reply to our response and write "Privacy appeal". We will respond to appeals within 60 days and explain our decision. If you're still not satisfied, you may contact your state Attorney General.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We don't track visitors across sites or sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. We nonetheless honor Global Privacy Control signals as a valid opt-out request.

Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, such as Formspree and GitHub. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
